## 🚀 Project Overview
An end-to-end business intelligence suite delivering deep analytics on revenue growth, customer segmentation, operational performance, and regional logistics across multiple markets.

## 📈 Key Features & Insights
- **Financial Tracking:** Real-time monitoring of Net Profit, Profit Margins, Subtotals, Tax, and Shipping Costs.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Value-based categorization (VIP, Regular, New, Loyal) driving targeted marketing.
- **Logistics & Returns:** Granular tracking of order statuses and return drivers across major cities.
